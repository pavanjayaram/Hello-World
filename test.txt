lsof -i :9001 | awk 'NR!=1 {print $2}' | xargs -r kill -9

lsof -i :9001: Lists all open files/processes running on port 9001.
awk 'NR!=1 {print $2}': Skips the header row (NR!=1) and extracts just the Process ID (PID) which is the second column ($2).
xargs -r kill -9: Takes those PIDs and force kills them (kill -9).
